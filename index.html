Fundacja Twarze Depresji

The "Faces of Depression" Foundation fights the stigma around depression in Poland. It runs awareness campaigns, supports people living with depression and their families, and helps them find professional help.

  • Free remote psychological help and psychiatric consultations
  • A psychological counselling centre in Warsaw
  • Poland's longest-running campaign against the stigma of depression

All donations go directly to the foundation, not to me.
